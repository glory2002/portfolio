The challenge wasn't content. It was coordinating learning, reviews, and progression.

Tanya needed more than a website with lessons. She needed a system where students could learn step by step, submit work, receive feedback, repeat corrections, and eventually graduate.

The core challenge was designing a clear workflow across students, courses, levels, reviews, and statuses — without making the platform feel complex.

From trainer-led teaching to an independent digital academy

Tanya had been teaching through an existing academy model, but wanted to build a platform under her own name, with her own curriculum, students, and review process.

The platform had to support her existing way of teaching, not force her into a generic LMS structure.

One platform. Three different mental models.

Each role interacted with the same system, but from a completely different perspective.

01

Student

Learns, submits assignments, tracks progress.

02

Trainer

Reviews work, gives feedback, approves or returns levels.

03

Creates courses, manages students, publishes content.

Product decisions that shaped the experience

01

Courses are built in levels

Because PMU training requires step-by-step progression and trainer approval before students move forward.

02

Reviews live inside the learning flow

Because feedback is part of the education, not a separate messaging feature.

03

Statuses are visible, but simplified

Because the trainer needs operational clarity, while students need reassurance.

04

Mobile first, iPad comfortable

Because students mostly learn on phones, while trainers review better on larger screens.

Exploring the workflow through low-fidelity screens

I used wireframes to test whether the structure could support all roles, edge cases, and status changes before investing in UI detail.

Role-based experiences

The same platform, built to feel right for three completely different people using it.

The previews are screen recordings from the live platform — not prototypes or concept mockups.

Student experience

Students log in, continue their course, submit work, and see where they stand. The interface is calm and focused — one step at a time, no noise around what matters next.

Trainer experience

The trainer sees who needs attention, reviews submitted work, and gives feedback. Everything is organised around the review queue — so nothing gets missed and the workflow stays efficient.

Course builder experience

The trainer can create and publish courses independently. Levels, assignments, and content are structured in one place — no technical knowledge required.

Built for the phone.
Comfortable on the iPad.

Students follow their courses on the go — most of them on mobile. Trainers tend to review and approve on iPad, where there's more room to see everything at once. The platform works well on both, without needing a separate version for each.
