Challenge

The problem

PHI Academy + Craft Master worked. Not her brand. Not her rules. She needed her own platform.

  • No real control over content or UX.
  • Feedback manual, untracked.
  • Everything under someone else's brand.
  • No own certs, progress, or enrolment.

Goals

What needed to be built

01

Independent platform

Own branding, domain, and content — no third-party lock-in.

02

Structured progression

Levels with clear status so students always know what's next.

03

Direct feedback

Per-level chat: submit work, get corrections in real time.

04

Automated certification

Branded certificates generated when a course is completed.

Key takeaways

Levels work. They need a real feedback loop for skills.

Most tools skip mentorship. Direct instructor access wins.

Rare combo: learning + feedback + branded certs.

Opportunity: All three — built for Tanya's method.

Concept

The solution

I designed a custom LMS that enables structured progression, real-time feedback, and certification through a dual role system.

The platform is built around two core roles with distinct responsibilities.

Instructor

Tanya Hristova · admin surface

  • Author courses & levels
  • Invite students
  • Review work in chat
  • Issue certificates
Student

Enrolled learner · academy surface

  • Join via invite
  • Progress level by level
  • Submit work · chat feedback
  • Download certificate

Reflection

What I learned

Systems UX: interlocking states, not pages. Level status model = linchpin.

Three-panel layout = density vs. calm. Many passes to feel right in real study sessions.

Tech literacy helped: realtime, RLS, schema ↔ UI.

Next: richer student dashboard — at-a-glance progress to completion.
