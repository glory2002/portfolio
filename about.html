Hi there, I'm Gloria!

Born and raised in Bulgaria, I now live in Eindhoven, the Netherlands.
I grew up between art and engineering — my mother is an art teacher and my father is an engineer — and I later graduated in Engineering Design. Early on, I understood the difference between art and design: an artist solves their own problems, while a designer solves the problems of others.

Today, my focus is UI/UX, but my previous experience gives me a broader perspective as a designer. My approach is shaped by different disciplines: logic from product design, aesthetics from graphic design, and empathy from digital user experience. This helps me create interfaces that are useful, meaningful, and emotionally engaging.

My motivation comes from challenges and change — they wake up my mind and push me to find the best solutions.

When I'm not working, you can find me playing with my child, doing folk embroidery, practicing paneurhythmy, or spending time in nature.
