About me

I translate complexity into clear, usable experiences.

Being a designer, for me, is like being a translator — between business, technology, and people. I'm drawn to that hybrid space, and to the challenge of finding the simplest answer to a genuinely complex problem.

Outside of screens, I do folk embroidery and practice paneurithmy. Both are old, both are rhythmic, and both — somehow — feel a lot like design.
