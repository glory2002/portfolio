Hi there, I'm Gloria!

I grew up between art and engineering — my mother is an art teacher and my father is an engineer. Early on, I understood the difference between art and design: an artist solves their own problems, while a designer solves the problems of others.

Today my focus is UI/UX, but my previous experience gives me a broader perspective as a designer. My approach is shaped by different disciplines: logic from product design, aesthetics from graphic design, and empathy from digital user experience. This allows me to create interfaces that are beautiful, meaningful, and emotionally engaging.
